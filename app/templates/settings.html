Job-alert e-mails

LinkedIn, AllJobs and Drushim send you job alerts; with every search the console reads them from your mailbox (the last {{ mail_days }} days) and shows the jobs under New Opportunities → From alerts. Employers' replies to your applications are read too: a confirmation sets the record to Applied, a no to Rejected by them (listed under From alerts → Replies). The mailbox is opened read-only: nothing is marked, moved or deleted.
